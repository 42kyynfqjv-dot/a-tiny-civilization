# World presentation policy

Status: accepted project policy.

The simulation can contain birth, injury, predation, conflict, death, and extinction as restrained
mechanical facts. The public observatory never presents sexual activity, reproductive mechanism,
graphic violence, gore, or sensationalized suffering. Public birth records omit reproductive
partners, private development, sex mechanism, and parentage detail. Public death records use neutral,
non-graphic language and may group or withhold detail when an event would be exploitative or unsafe
to display.

Supporter aliases do not turn an organism into a protected character. A named organism may become
injured or die, and the world may become extinct. The interface must disclose this before purchase
and avoid celebratory payment language around a death. Observer summaries are deterministic finding
aids grounded in cited events, not invented dialogue, motives, feelings, or dramatic narration.

The inhabitants are bounded computational agents. The project does not claim consciousness,
sentience, human-equivalent experience, or moral personhood. It also does not use that disclaimer to
justify gratuitous presentation: public outputs remain restrained, factual, and suitable for a broad
audience. Public audit endpoints expose payload-free commitments. Any more detailed technical
verification material must pass the same presentation review and must not turn private mechanisms
into explicit public spectacle.
