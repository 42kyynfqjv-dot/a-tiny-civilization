# ADR 0109: Durable fauna individuals start with source-ranged tetrapods

Date: 2026-08-08

Status: Accepted

## Context

The first mechanically passing canonical candidate selected fauna uniformly from every animal
range record at the committed origin. Twenty-four of its 32 selected taxa were insects, while every
selected taxon received the same provisional individual body machinery. That draw was deterministic,
but it confused source-backed ecological presence with sufficient evidence and mechanics for a
durable individual life. It also made observer biographies and lineage events imply a precision the
world did not possess.

## Decision

- Seeded fauna selection schema two defines the policy
  `ranged-tetrapod-individuals-v1`.
- The policy filters the source-confirmed local candidate set to Amphibia, Aves, Mammalia, and
  Reptilia before applying a new seed-bound ranking domain. It does not use an authored species
  allowlist and does not reroll the public world seed.
- Selected tetrapods may receive durable founder identities. Other source-ranged animals remain
  ecological evidence until a versioned cohort or life-stage representation can model them without
  fabricated individual physiology.
- The policy does not assert local abundance, native status, habitat suitability, or complete
  ecological coverage. Those remain scientific-admission questions.
- Schema-one selection and its ranking domain remain supported exactly for archived replay.

## Consequences

Durable animal biographies, bodies, births, deaths, and lineages begin with a defensible identity
tier instead of whichever animal groups dominate the source catalog. The source pool remains broad,
but finer ecological organisms can enter canonical causality only through an explicit representation
policy. The same committed seed and origin are preserved; changing the identity policy creates a new
candidate artifact, not a curated replacement seed.
