# ADR 0041: Provisional embodied activity executes at the partition barrier

## Status

Accepted on 2026-08-07 for new provisional integration worlds only. It is not a
scientific admission of behaviour, metabolism, ecology, or cognition.

## Context

Ruleset two proved that every living embodied organism receives deterministic work,
but emitted only an age increment. Ruleset three adds a source-pinned celestial input,
yet the live integration world still has no organism-owned perception or action in its
canonical history. That makes the observatory truthfully quiet, but leaves the motor
and sensory contracts unexercised in the actual scheduled path.

## Decision

- Ruleset four preserves the ruleset-two age transition and the ruleset-three DE441
  input requirement.
- Each scheduled living organism additionally receives one label-free
  `body_clock_phase` interoceptive reading and one closed-grammar primitive action.
- The action is chosen by a stable function of the organism identity and durable age
  tick. It can only be `rest`, `orient`, `reach`, or `emit_signal`; it does not claim
  an environmental effect, an affordance, knowledge, culture, or intention.
- The events are emitted in fixed per-organism order after the age transition, then
  merged by the existing partition barrier. Replay, snapshots, event budgets, and
  source-backed celestial hand-off retain their prior invariants.
- Existing worlds never change ruleset. A world that began with ruleset three remains
  clock-only; a later integration world must be initialized with ruleset four.
- Public projections continue to withhold raw perception/action mechanism detail.

## Consequences

This makes the body-to-perception-to-action route executable and auditable without
pretending that the project has solved local physics. The next ruleset must replace
the placeholder motor cadence with source-pinned local environmental readings and
resolved physical effects: movement, energy exchange, fatigue, injury, and material
state. Reproduction, population ecology, learned memory, communication, artifacts,
and culture remain separate work.
