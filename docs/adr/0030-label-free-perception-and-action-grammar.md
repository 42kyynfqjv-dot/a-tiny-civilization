# ADR 0030: Agent inputs use label-free readings and a closed primitive action grammar

## Status

Accepted on 2026-08-06. The domain contracts are implemented; durable embodied events
and species-specific execution follow the scientific parameter and scheduler work.

## Decision

- A need signal names only a bodily pressure: energy deficit, hydration deficit,
  thermal discomfort, pain, or fatigue. It does not identify a solution.
- A situated perception consists of bounded, ordered property readings through a
  sensory channel. Codes may refer to physical dimensions such as temperature or
  surface roughness, but reject affordances and cultural conclusions including food,
  tool, weapon, medicine, technology, paper, and writing.
- The closed action grammar is move, orient, reach, grasp, release, apply force, bite,
  chew, swallow, rest, and emit signal. Effects are resolved by embodied physics; no
  action declares a cultural purpose such as building, knapping, cooking, hunting, or
  writing.
- Reproductive physiology, injury, and mortality remain internal neutral mechanics.
  Public projection policy remains restrained and non-explicit.

## Consequences

The engine can give organisms evolutionary bodily capability without telling them what
objects are for. Cultural discoveries must be learned from observed effects, memory,
imitation, and communication. Before a canonical world, these contracts must be added
to versioned durable perception/action events and tied to sourced species parameters.
