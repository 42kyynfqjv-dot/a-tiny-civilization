# ADR 0049: Experimental genesis precedes exhaustive scientific admission

## Status

Accepted on 2026-08-07. This supersedes earlier statements that every engineering-
assumption profile is categorically ineligible for a public genesis.

## Context

The next world must be an honest emergent-civilization experiment, but exhaustive
cross-disciplinary admission of a full Earth, all participating organisms, and every
causal parameter would delay testing the complete system without making an isolated
layer more trustworthy. The project owner accepts that the next world is an experiment
rather than a scientifically validated Earth model.

Scientific deferral must not become silent fabrication. Real-world identity,
licensing, provenance, deterministic replay, public-safe presentation, and the
observer boundary remain non-negotiable.

## Decision

- The next public world may pass the quality-world gate while its coupled scientific
  inputs remain provisional. It is labelled **experimental** and
  **not scientifically admitted** everywhere status is presented.
- Every species, material, and geographic entity still references its actual
  real-world identity. Every retained source is cited and content-addressed. Every
  unsupported causal parameter is classified as an engineering assumption in a
  published assumption ledger.
- Licensing, canonical-byte validation, source integrity, unit/range checks, replay,
  privacy, security, memory/model isolation, and operational readiness remain launch
  gates. Exhaustive literature review, uncertainty propagation, ecological
  calibration, and cross-layer scientific admission do not.
- Genesis pins the exact provisional profiles and source artifacts that shape the
  world. Later scientific findings cannot tune, rescue, or rewrite that history; they
  are applied only to an explicitly authorized successor world.
- The observatory may report what the experimental world did. It must not describe
  its outputs as scientifically accurate predictions about people, animals, ecology,
  or civilization.

## Consequences

Implementation can reach a real public genesis before the full scientific program is
complete without hiding what is assumed. The next world's value is its auditable,
uninterrupted history. The successor-world process becomes the mechanism for improving
scientific fidelity.
