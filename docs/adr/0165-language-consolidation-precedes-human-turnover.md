# ADR 0165: endogenous language consolidation precedes human turnover

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: Accepted

## Context

The first compositional driver correctly allowed every ordered call sequence from
genesis, but production consulted an exact call pair whenever a preceding call was
present. A learner therefore ignored a strong atomic convention when the exact pair
was unfamiliar. Thirty-two calls created 1,024 immediate pair contexts: the public
world repeatedly formed credible conventions and then lost them as evidence was
fragmented across combinations.

The observer also needs a clear explanation for the absence of human births and
deaths during the initial language experiment. Fauna must retain ordinary ecological
turnover, and no observer detector may become a simulation input.

## Decision

Ruleset 42 makes the following replay-stable changes from genesis:

- A compositional production context inherits the producer's strongest atomic
  convention when no stronger exact pair exists. Composition extends learned calls;
  it does not erase them.
- Hearing the same physical form from independent human sources within one simulated
  day adds a bounded reinforcement bonus. One prolific caller cannot create this
  social support alone.
- A recently heard form keeps its neutral imitation advantage when unclassified or
  when it predicts the producer's resolved action. Once the learner has assigned it
  a conflicting grounded meaning, it is not echoed alongside that conflicting action.
  Alternative grounded production remains available as a physical repair response.
- The human life cycle opens only while canonical private learning state contains at
  least three distinct grounded motor meanings, each held by at least four living
  people. This is computed inside the deterministic state and never reads the public
  language projection or its editorial thresholds.
- Before that boundary, human lethal loads remain one physical unit below failure and
  new human reproductive developments do not start. Existing developments would
  still finish. Fauna mortality and reproduction are never gated by human language.

No call, meaning, word, grammar, communicative objective, writing system, or artifact
use is supplied. All thirty-two calls and every composition remain available from
genesis. The ruleset reuses the compositional event, state-hash, and snapshot schemas
because it changes deterministic policy only and adds no canonical fields.

## Consequences

- Atomic conventions can compound into compositions instead of being reset by them.
- Cross-person agreement has more weight than repeated exposure to one source.
- Human births and deaths become a visible consequence of inhabitants crossing their
  own shared-convention boundary; ecology continues independently.
- The public detector may cross its stricter provenance threshold later than the
  private life-cycle boundary. Neither can feed observer labels back into history.
- Language remains contingent. The change removes a convergence trap but does not
  guarantee a vocabulary, grammar, writing, or civilization.
