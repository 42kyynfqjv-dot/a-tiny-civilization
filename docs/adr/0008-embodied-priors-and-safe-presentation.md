# ADR 0008: Bodies have evolved priors; public presentation is non-explicit

## Status

Accepted on 2026-08-06.

## Context

A causally open simulation still needs a defensible boundary between innate bodily
capability and learned knowledge. Making every motor and physiological operation a
chance discovery would be less faithful to real organisms and would turn early
history into arbitrary failure. Encoding food classifications, reproductive concepts,
or social practices as instincts would erase much of the emergence the project exists
to observe.

The world can also contain reproduction, injury, predation, and death without making
sexual or violent material part of the observer experience.

## Decision

- Species receive sourced, species-appropriate physiological regulation, reflexes,
  drives, and primitive motor capabilities.
- A need signal and a primitive action are distinct from knowledge of a solution. A
  person may feel hunger and be physically able to grasp, bite, chew, and swallow, but
  is not told that a perceived object is edible or how it should be acquired, prepared,
  stored, or shared.
- Reproductive physiology may operate without agents beginning with concepts such as
  fertility, parenthood, heredity, kinship, marriage, or a causal explanation of
  reproduction. Courtship, partner choice, childcare strategies, and institutions are
  learned or emergent.
- Innate parameters are versioned by real species or life stage, scientifically sourced
  where possible, and listed as assumptions where evidence is absent.
- Canonical sensitive mechanics use neutral physical state and versioned mechanism
  codes, never generated or graphic prose.
- Public projections never show, animate, or describe sexual acts or violence. They may
  expose only restrained outcomes necessary to understand history, such as pregnancy,
  birth, injury, death, and population change.
- Observer filtering changes presentation only. It cannot remove causal evidence from
  the append-only log or change perceptions and consequences inside the world.

## Consequences

Discovery remains focused on ecologically and culturally meaningful uncertainty rather
than rediscovery of basic body operation. The observer application needs an explicit
safe projection vocabulary and tests proving that raw sensitive mechanism detail does
not enter public templates or API fields.
