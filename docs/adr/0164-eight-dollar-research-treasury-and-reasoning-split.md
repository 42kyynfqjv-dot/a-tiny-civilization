# ADR 0164: Eight-dollar research treasury and reasoning split

Status: accepted

## Decision

The cancer-research treasury hard stop is raised from USD 3 to USD 8, with a
USD 7.50 operating target. The deterministic adapter remains responsible for
mechanical discriminating screens and frozen-plan independent replications.
Hypothesis formation, interpretation, literature challenge, and machine or
treatment design continue through the inference ladder. The free experimental
Hetzner Qwen mixture-of-experts route remains first. Fireworks remains the
explicitly budget-capped paid tail when the free providers do not return a
usable result.

Cancer research does not use the host's CPU-only local route under policy 12.
The measured latency would turn it into a throughput bottleneck, while sharing
it would also make public-world cognition contend with long research prompts.
Policy 11 remains readable for durable attempts started before the split.

## Consequences

The treasury is a circuit breaker, not a throughput target. Hetzner is free
during its experimental phase and therefore does not consume it. Actual paid
duration varies with prompt and completion lengths, failures, and the mix of
research tasks.

The database migration only upgrades an account still carrying the prior exact
limits, so operator changes are not silently overwritten.
