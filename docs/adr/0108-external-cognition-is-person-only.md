# ADR 0108: External cognition is person-only

Date: 2026-08-08

Status: Accepted

## Context

The ruleset-25 canonical candidate began with two people and 64 fauna individuals. World-total
cognition selected uniformly from every living organism, so its first successful local Qwen receipt
went to the moth `Chlumetia transversa`. This is mechanically deterministic but allocates the scarce
cognition treasury away from the civilization experiment and makes cost scale with fauna identity
choices. Fauna already have deterministic embodied policies, bodily learning, signaling,
reproduction, and heredity; model access is not required for them to remain causal lives.

## Decision

- Ruleset 26 admits only living `person` organisms to scheduled external cognition.
- The engine constructs the complete stable-ID-ordered eligible set from canonical state before the
  seed-bound subject draw. Infrastructure still cannot pick a person.
- Directly planning or replaying a fauna cognition selection under ruleset 26 fails validation.
- Fauna mechanics, identities, memories, projections, and supporter eligibility are otherwise
  unchanged.
- Ruleset-25 selection and replay remain byte-identical.

## Consequences

Free and paid model capacity now serves the civilization rather than the number of individually
represented animals. Adding fauna cannot dilute a person's chance of receiving a bounded cognition
request or raise model spend. Fauna remain active deterministic organisms rather than decorative
observer records.
