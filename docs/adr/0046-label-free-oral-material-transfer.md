# ADR 0046: Oral material transfer is physical, species-bound, and label-free

## Status

Accepted on 2026-08-07. Ruleset-twelve implementation follows this contract.

## Context

Ruleset eleven can choose the innate bite, chew, and swallow motions but intentionally
does not assign them a useful outcome. Survival needs a causal path by which situated
experience can eventually distinguish material effects. That path must not tell an
organism what is food or water, infer a response from a modern material name, consume
an unmeasured infinite object, or make replay recalculate scientific inputs.

## Decision

- A material instance may atomically retain a positive physical mass and a strictly
  species-ordered set of oral-transfer commitments. Each commitment binds the exact
  cited real material, cited real species, evidence class, evidence-profile digest,
  indivisible transfer mass, recoverable energy, and hydration-load recovery.
- Version one is deliberately narrow. It models only exact energy and hydration
  recovery. It does not claim digestion, toxicity, choking, injury, micronutrients,
  taste learning, or disease; those require separate source-backed causal state.
- The deterministic action policy never reads whether a matching commitment exists or
  what effect it contains. A held object remains a candidate for the same primitive
  motions with or without a beneficial response. The organism receives only the later
  bodily consequences through its ordinary need state.
- A swallow motion with a held target produces a mass-transfer event only when an exact
  species-matched commitment exists. The transfer must consume one whole committed
  portion, preserve integer mass exactly, and occur between the matching action and
  that organism's same-tick bodily transition. Depleted material identity remains in
  canonical history but cannot be held or targeted again.
- Energy and hydration recovery are applied after that tick's metabolic and hydration
  exposure and before mechanical mortality. The exact resolved mass event and bodily
  transition are recorded; replay never samples, looks up, or recomputes the profile.
- Event schema fourteen and snapshot/state-hash schema fifteen isolate this semantic
  boundary. Earlier rulesets and histories retain their published bytes.

## Consequences

An organism can now accidentally obtain relief from a real material without receiving
the conclusion that it is edible, potable, safe, or desirable. Repeated experience and
learned action values remain a later checkpoint. Engineering-assumption fixtures prove
the mechanism but are ineligible for public genesis until their material/species
profiles pass the integrated scientific admission process.
